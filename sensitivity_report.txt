RETIREMENT RECOMMENDATION SENSITIVITY
====================================

Cases: 27
Baseline recommendation: age 56
Recommended-age range: 56 to 67
Most common recommendation: age 56 (18 of 27 cases)
Confidence classification: Low (range exceeds 2 years)

Distribution:
- Age 56: 18 cases
- Age 58: 3 cases
- Age 67: 6 cases

Interpretation:
The baseline recommendation of age 56 is the modal result, but it is not
robust across the full tested assumption grid. Lower pension growth combined
with lower ISA growth pushes the recommendation to age 67 in several cases.
A 4% pension-growth assumption with 3% ISA growth produces age 58.

The sensitivity analysis is informational only and does not alter the live
recommendation.
